**Slay the Spire 2 Companion** is a curated list of Slay the Spire 2 open-source tools:

## ✨ Features

### ESP
- Draw Order Viewer — visualize card draw order
- Enemy Intent Display — show planned enemy moves
- Replay Notes — record and review combat runs
- Speed Control — adjust combat speed for study (0.1x to 5x)

### Wallhack
- Card Catalog — browse all cards, upgrades, and rarities
- Relic Tracker — organize relic notes
- Potion Manager — view potion effects and drop rates

### Aimbot
- Map Path Notes — segment study on any act layout
- Energy Display — track current energy and draw pile
- Buffer Notes — chain complex card combos in the editor
